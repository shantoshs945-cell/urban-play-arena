OUR STORY
ABOUT US

Welcome to Urban Play Arena, Kakinada's premier sports destination. Founded in 2021 by PBSSD Prasad, Urban Play Arena was created with a vision to bring sports enthusiasts together in a world-class environment that promotes fitness, teamwork, and healthy competition. Our facility features premium Box Cricket and Football turfs designed for performance, safety, and an exceptional playing experience. With high-quality artificial turf and professional floodlights, players can enjoy the game both day and night.

Our Vision

To inspire a healthier and more active lifestyle in Kakinada by providing a top-quality sporting environment where players of all skill levels can play, compete, and grow.
